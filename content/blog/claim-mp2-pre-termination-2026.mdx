Planning to withdraw your MP2 before the 5-year lock-in period? This is a guide for a pre-termination of a 5-year lock-in period account.

Here are your options:

## Option 1: Online via Virtual Pag-IBIG

1. Visit the official Pag-IBIG Fund website
2. Click **"Virtual Pag-IBIG"**
3. Select **"Claim Pag-IBIG Savings"**
4. Choose **"MP2 Savings Maturity"**

Prepare the following:

- Accomplished & signed [Claim Application Form (HQP-PFF-285)](https://www.pagibigfund.gov.ph/documents/PFF285_ApplicationProvidentBenefitsClaim.pdf) — check **"Pre-termination"** and indicate reason **"Personal Reason"**
- Clear copy of ONE (1) valid government ID
- Loyalty Card Plus (if available)
- Selfie holding your valid ID (and LCP if applicable)

Then enter your MID number, click **"Validate,"** and upload all required documents.

> If you don't have a Loyalty Card Plus, choose **"Release through Check"** instead.

## Option 2: Walk-in at the Nearest Pag-IBIG Branch

Bring:

- 1 original signed Application for Provident Benefits (HQP-PFF-285)
- 1 photocopy of a valid ID (bring the original for verification)

**Notes:**

- Originals must be presented for authentication
- Office hours: Monday–Friday, 8:00 AM–5:00 PM
- Filing through a representative? Bring 1 original Authorization Letter plus valid IDs of both parties

## My Personal Experience

I chose Option 2 since a branch was near us — no long lines, just get a queue number and say you're withdrawing MP2. I don't have a Loyalty Card, so I opted for check release.

Processing time was **20–27 working days**. I received an SMS once it was ready for pickup — don't forget your Provident Benefits Acknowledgement Receipt upon claiming. I deposited my check via GoTyme Bank's mobile deposit.

For concerns, email **contactus@pagibigfund.gov.ph** — they're responsive.

## When Pre-Termination Is Allowed

Pre-termination of MP2 Savings is allowed under the following circumstances:

1. Total disability or insanity
2. Separation from service due to health reasons
3. Death of the member or immediate family member
4. Retirement (except if already a pensioner/retiree)
5. Permanent departure from the country
6. Unemployment due to layoff and/or company closure
7. Critical illness of the member or immediate family member (certified by a licensed physician, subject to approval of Pag-IBIG Fund)
8. Repatriation of an OFW member from their host country
9. Other meritorious grounds approved by Pag-IBIG Fund

A couple of fee notes worth knowing:

- If you chose **compounded MP2 dividends** and pre-terminate for reasons other than #1–9 above, you'll be charged a 50% pre-termination fee on total dividends earned. Current-year dividends also carry a 50% fee and get released separately, as a second release.
- If you chose **annual dividend payout** and pre-terminate for reasons other than #1–9 above, the same 50% fee applies on total dividends earned, deducted from your principal savings — with current-year dividends again released separately.
