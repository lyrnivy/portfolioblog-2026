A same-day DIY hike to Mt. Masungi and Nagpatong Rock, mapped out from meetup to arrival back in Manila. Here's the exact schedule we followed:

## Itinerary

- **01:00 AM** — Meetup at Cubao Jollibee (Farmers)
- **04:00 AM** — Arrival at Brgy. Hall of Cuyambay, registration
- **05:00 AM** — Start trek
- **08:10 AM** — Arrival at the summit (Billboard) of Mt. Masungi
- **11:30 AM** — Arrival at Nagpatong Rock — rest, lunch, photos
- **06:29 PM** — Back in Manila

## Getting There (DIY route)

From Manila, hanap kayo ng jeep na may signboard na **Cogeo — P25.00**, then continue with additional jeepney rides that run roughly P45–P100 per segment depending on where you're transferring. All in, budget **not more than P600 balikan** for the whole hike.

## What to Bring

- Water (more than you think you need)
- Trail snacks + packed lunch
- First aid kit
- Flashlight
- Extra clothes

## A Reminder

Please do not litter on the mountains, as respect to our nature. Leave it better than you found it — the view is worth protecting.
