## Visitor Visa for Filipinos (2025)

Philippine passport holders **need a Visitor Visa** to enter New Zealand — the NZeTA (electronic travel authority) is for visa-waiver countries only, and the Philippines is not on that list. Apply online through Immigration New Zealand: [immigration.govt.nz](https://www.immigration.govt.nz) — the [Visitor Visa page](https://www.immigration.govt.nz/new-zealand-visas/visas/visa/visitor-visa) has the full checklist.

- **Fee** — the online Visitor Visa application starts at **NZD $441**, which includes the NZD $100 International Visitor Conservation and Tourism Levy (IVL). Fees change, so confirm the current amount on the official page before you apply.
- **VFS Global service fee** — passports are submitted and collected through VFS Global in Makati or Cebu; their service fee is roughly ₱1,500 on top of the visa fee.
- **Maximum stay** — up to 9 months within an 18-month period (single stays are granted for 6 or 9 months).
- **Passport** — valid for at least 3 months beyond the date you plan to leave New Zealand.
- **Proof of funds** — at least **NZD $1,000 per month** of your stay if you're self-funding, or **NZD $400 per month** if your accommodation is already paid for. Show bank statements to match.
- **Onward travel** — a ticket out of New Zealand, or evidence you can afford one.
- **Supporting documents** — travel itinerary, proof of accommodation, proof of employment and approved leave (or proof of funds if not employed), and anything showing ties that bring you home.
- **Processing** — Immigration NZ aims to decide 80% of visitor visa applications within about 2 weeks; apply well ahead of a Christmas trip since that's peak season.

## Getting There

There are no direct Manila–Auckland flights on my usual budget, so I route through Australia: **Manila → Brisbane (sometimes Sydney) → Auckland**. Round-trip runs around **₱50,000** on Qantas, which is the airline I recommend — they're generous with points, so the miles add up fast across this kind of long-haul routing.

One thing to check if you transit through Australia: Philippine passport holders usually need an **Australian transit visa (subclass 771)** even to connect airside. It's free, but apply for it ahead of time.

## Getting Around

To do trips outside the cities you really need a car, or it's best to book a group tour (or a DIY tour package). Public transport between towns is limited.

Within a city you can take buses or trains, but the intervals are long — often close to an hour between services depending on the city. In Auckland we used the **AT HOP card** for buses and trains; you can buy one at pretty much any convenience store, then top it up as you go.

## What to Budget

Prepare **at least ₱60,000**, more if you're covering your own accommodation (my parents handled housing this time). New Zealand is expensive day to day — a basic meal like fish and chips or eggs benedict starts around **USD $15**, and that's the low end. Groceries and cooking help a lot on a longer stay.

## Final Thoughts

Auckland over Christmas is long, bright summer days and an easy base for the North Island. The visa is the real work — start it early, get your proof of funds in order, and sort the Australian transit visa if you route through Brisbane or Sydney. Once you're there, budget for a rental car or tours, because the sights are spread out and the buses are slow.
