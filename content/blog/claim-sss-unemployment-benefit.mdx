This is not a loan! It's a cash benefit that will be credited straight to your bank savings. No need to go to a branch to claim — all of it is done online.

## Step 1. Log in to your My.SSS Account

Head to [www.sss.gov.ph](https://www.sss.gov.ph) and enter your login credentials.

## Step 2. Register your disbursement account

Look up **Services > Disbursement Account**. This is where they'll deposit the cash claim once it's approved by DOLE — it can be a bank or an e-wallet account.

## Step 3. Fill out the application form

After your disbursement account is approved, go to the **Benefits** tab **> Unemployment Benefit** and fill out the application form.

> This is the crucial step. If you're not sure about the details below, contact your HR before filling it out — it might get denied if the records don't match your DOLE branch.

## Step 4. Wait for the notification to your previous office

After you submit your claim for Unemployment Benefit, it will first notify your previous office. Take note of your **transaction number** and **preferred DOLE branch** (found on your Notifications tab), and send these details to your HR office contact.

## Step 5. Apply for your DOLE/DMW e-Certification

Once HR verifies the claim, you can apply for a DOLE/DMW e-Certification of Involuntary Separation. Only three requirements:

1. Notice of Termination of Employment issued by the employer — or a duly notarized Affidavit of Termination of Employment if you don't have one
2. Transaction reference from SSS
3. Valid ID with signature

For NCR-based employees, file at [clients.ncr.dole.gov.ph/isc](https://clients.ncr.dole.gov.ph/isc/). For Cebu-based employees, email **ro7@dole.gov.ph** or **tcfodolecertification@gmail.com**.

Processing time is five working days.

## Step 6. Wait for the payout

Give it 5–10 working days for the cash benefit to reflect in your bank or e-wallet account. They'll notify you by email too.

**Note:** if you've been unemployed for more than one year, your claim might be denied.
