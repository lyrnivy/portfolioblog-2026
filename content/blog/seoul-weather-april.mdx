## What's the Weather Like in Seoul in April?

<div className="mb-6">
  <LiteTikTok videoId="7492594674079632660" caption="Seoul, April 13, 2025 — weather check" />
</div>

A combination of rain and cold. It's chilly in the mornings, and then in the afternoon — when the temperature actually drops — that's when it tends to rain, depending on the day. Pack for it:

- Thermals
- A warm jacket and a coat
- An umbrella or rain jacket

For checking conditions I used the **Apple Weather app**, which was pretty accurate the whole trip. I also used **Naver Map** — it has live CCTV/traffic-camera feeds, so you can pull up an area and see the actual sky before you head out.

## Cherry Blossoms at Seokchon Lake

<div className="mb-6">
  <LiteTikTok videoId="7494832832532008212" caption="Cherry blossoms at Seokchon Lake" />
</div>

Mid-April is still cherry blossom season, so pack a few nice outfits and head to **Seokchon Lake** (석촌호수), the walking loop right beside Lotte World in Jamsil. The blossoms ring the whole lake and it's free.

## Why We Went: 2NE1's Live Encore

The whole reason for the trip was 2NE1's "Pretty Boy" live encore — watching them perform has been a childhood dream of mine. It was so good, and the live band was excellent. It was a short stay built around this one night, and worth it.

## What to Pack for April

- Thermals, a warm jacket, and a coat — mornings are cold, afternoons can turn rainy
- Umbrella or packable rain jacket
- One or two nice outfits for cherry blossom photos
- Comfortable shoes for the Seokchon Lake loop
