## Before You Fly: Visit Japan Web

Before you go, fill out [Visit Japan Web](https://www.vjw.digital.go.jp/main/#/vjwplo001) — the official site for pre-registering your immigration and customs declarations online. Do it a few days ahead of your flight, save the QR codes it generates, and you can skip the paper forms and move through the airport faster on arrival.

## The Itinerary

### Day 1 (July 23) — Arrival in Osaka

Settled into the hotel, ¥190 transfer from Nankai to Honmachi. Arrived around 7 PM at Garner Hotel Osaka Honmachi (¥2,889/night x 3 nights). Grabbed dinner at a nearby konbini and rested.

### Day 2 (July 24)

<LiteYoutube videoId="Tcklp3weqcY" title="Osaka Camera Hunt + TeamLabs Botanical Garden 🌸" />

Looked around for a Ricoh IV / Seiko at nearby camera shops, then Yodobashi and Bic Camera in Namba. Ended the day at teamLab Botanical Garden Osaka, 7:45 PM.

### Day 3 (July 25) — Tenjin Matsuri

<LiteYoutube videoId="bS09zJ0ALUY" title="Osaka Tenjin Matsuri, Dotonbori Foodtrip - Festival and Fireworks 2026" />

Slow morning, withdrew cash at 7-Eleven. Late lunch at Dotonbori around 1 PM — takoyaki, of course. Caught Tenjin Matsuri: the land procession at 3 PM and fireworks over the Okawa River from 6–9 PM.

### Day 4 (July 26) — Osaka to Kyoto

<LiteYoutube videoId="l2s2Jiwga4I" title="Going to Kyoto from Osaka, Hotel & Cafe Restaurants Nearby ✨🇯🇵" />

Packed up and checked out before 11 AM, ¥600 for transport to Kyoto. Checked into Prince Smart Inn. Visited Kyoto Aquarium when there was time (coffee and cake after, ¥2,105) and did some shopping at Don Quijote.

### Day 5 (July 27)

<LiteYoutube videoId="X597i1baH-Q" title="Kyoto Culture & Food Adventure: Chopsticks Making, Gion Food Tour & Mochi Experience! 🥢🍜🍡" />

Chopstick-making at 11 AM — ¥2,000 for engraving, ¥10,000 for knives. Stopped at a nearby café/mochi spot (~¥1,000). Visited Yasaka Pagoda, free entry.

### Day 6 (July 28)

<LiteYoutube videoId="mx4KwtU3Yug" title="Kyoto tea Ceremony Matcha Making + Arashiyama's Iconic Bamboo Forest 🍵🎋" />

Tea Ceremony & Matcha Making at 12 PM, with yukata rental on-site. Spent the rest of the day at Arashiyama Bamboo Grove, Togetsukyo Bridge, Nonomiya Shrine, and the Kimono Forest at Randen Station — all free, the same way most of the sights on my [Seoul trip](/blogs/seoul-trip-cost-breakdown-january) cost nothing. Dinner came out to ¥770.

### Day 7 (July 29)

<LiteYoutube videoId="ydFxagksBiQ" title="Kyoto Imperial Palace Walthrough plus Hojicha and Dior Shopping" />

Kyoto Imperial Palace, then a hoji-cha specialty store. Did some department store shopping and got the 5% discount plus tax refund.

### Day 8 (July 30) — Ohara Day Sanzen-in Temple

<LiteYoutube videoId="GwJxXXLOUkg" title="Sanzen-in Temple Ohara, Kyoto Walkthrough 2026" />

Left at 8:30 AM from hotel, reached Sanzen-in by 10AM. Spent the rest of the day on last-minute shopping and free time.

## Final Thoughts

Nine days across Osaka and Kyoto for about ₱48,000 — with a tea ceremony, a festival, and a botanical garden included — held up well for a summer trip. The two things that kept it affordable were a cheap promo flight and leaning on ¥30,000 of cash for food and local transport instead of eating out at restaurants every night. Kyoto's temples and Arashiyama cost nothing, so most days the only real spend was meals and one paid activity.
